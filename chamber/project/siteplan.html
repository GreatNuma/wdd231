Site Name & Branding

Site Name: Summit & Trail

Reasoning: "Summit & Trail" clearly communicates an active outdoor lifestyle brand focused on hiking, trail exploration, and nature conservation. It is concise, memorable, and evokes high-altitude adventure.

Optional Domain: summitandtrail.org

🏔️ Summit & Trail

Site Purpose

The purpose of Summit & Trail is to serve as a comprehensive local guide for outdoor enthusiasts. The site provides curated trail recommendations, difficulty ratings, essential gear checklists, and seasonal safety tips. Additionally, it offers a community contact form for user trail reports and newsletter subscriptions.

Target Audience & Scenarios

The primary audience includes local hikers, weekend warriors, trail runners, and outdoor visitors seeking reliable route information.

Scenario 1

"I'm planning a Saturday morning hike with my kids—where can I find easy-to-moderate trails with parking and restroom access?"

Scenario 2

"What essential gear and safety gear do I need to pack for a high-elevation summer day hike?"

Color Schema

The color palette is inspired by natural alpine landscapes: pine forests, earth tones, and warm trail markers.

Primary: Deep Forest Green #1B4332 Used for Header, Primary Buttons & Main Accent Borders
Secondary: Trail Amber #D97706 Used for Call-to-Actions, Links & Highlights
Background: Alpine Fog #F8FAFC Used for Main Page Background
Text: Slate Charcoal #1E293B Used for Headings & Body Text
