NP Membership Benefits

  • Free membership for registered non-profit organizations
  • Basic directory listing
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Monthly newsletter subscription

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Standard directory listing with business description
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Discounted admission to one luncheon per quarter
  • Monthly newsletter subscription

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Enhanced directory listing with logo and website link
  • Discounted admission to all chamber events
  • Quarterly mention in the chamber newsletter
  • Access to member-only training workshops
  • Social media shout-out from the chamber's official pages

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Premium directory listing, featured near the top
  • Eligibility for homepage "Member Spotlight" rotation
  • Free admission to all chamber events and the annual gala
  • Dedicated feature in the chamber newsletter
  • Priority access to training workshops and sponsorship opportunities
  • Premium advertising placement on the chamber website
